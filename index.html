Jodi Bragg

Public relations · social media · influencer marketing · organizational communications

I’m a communications strategist with more than 20 years of experience helping organizations communicate clearly, thoughtfully and effectively. My background spans corporate communications, public relations, social media, influencer marketing, brand reputation and organizational communications.

Over the course of my career, I’ve worked on or with more than 40 brands across multiple industry sectors and media-trained executives, physicians, authors and chefs. I’ve helped launch restaurants and products, managed teams, advised executives, built communications strategies, navigated compliance issues and translated complicated ideas into language people can actually understand.

I spent many years with AT&T and Cricket Wireless, where my work included communications strategy, social media, reputation management and the development of the company’s influencer marketing program. Success required close alignment with adjacent internal teams, strong agency management and using data to measure and demonstrate results. More recently, my work in education broadened my perspective on how people learn, communicate and connect—experience I now bring back to communications consulting.

I’m particularly good at seeing the connections between people, processes and ideas—and spotting opportunities that can get lost when everyone is focused on doing things the way they’ve always been done.

I hold a master’s degree in Political Communication from Louisiana State University and a bachelor’s degree in Corporate Journalism from Auburn University. I’ve also completed 60 hours of coursework in Organizational Coaching through the University of Georgia.
