Communications Strategist

Jodi Bragg

Communications · Public Relations · Digital Strategy

Jodi Bragg is a communications strategist with more than 20 years of experience spanning corporate communications, public relations, social media, influencer marketing, media relations and executive counsel.

Her career has included senior communications roles in corporate and agency environments, including AT&T, HL Group and Janine Gordon Associates, as well as experience in the United States Senate. She has advised organizations and leadership teams on communications strategy, reputation, stakeholder engagement, media relations, social compliance and translating complex business issues into clear, actionable communications.

At AT&T, Jodi helped launch and expand the company's Creator, Talent and Influencer program and worked across marketing, legal and agency teams on influencer strategy and FTC compliance. Earlier in her career, she directed communications and media strategy across consumer, healthcare, travel, financial services, nonprofit and public-affairs sectors.

Jodi holds a Master of Political Communication from Louisiana State University's Manship School of Mass Communication and a B.A. in Corporate Journalism from Auburn University.

Jodi currently works independently, providing strategic communications and advisory support to organizations and leadership teams.
