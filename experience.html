Experience

Communications
built on experience
—and insight.

For more than 20 years, Jodi Bragg has worked across strategic communications, public relations, social media, influencer marketing, reputation management and organizational communications—helping brands turn complicated ideas and challenges into messages people can act on and understand. Her perspective is informed by a natural curiosity in human behavior. Field experience and graduate-level coursework in organizational psychology and counseling have expanded the way she thinks about motivation, behavior, relationships and how people function within organizations.

Corporate communications

Strategy, influence and reputation.

AT&T + Cricket Wireless

At AT&T and Cricket Wireless, Jodi worked at the intersection of communications, social media, compliance and emerging influencer marketing.

She founded and led AT&T’s Creator, Talent and Influencer program, developing its strategy, processes and best practices before expanding the program to Cricket Wireless. Her work included influencer and talent relationships, campaign negotiation, contract coordination, FTC compliance, social strategy and collaboration across marketing and communications teams.

Earlier at AT&T, she led influencer relations and helped develop new ways for the brand to work with creators as social media became an increasingly important part of corporate communications.

Agency leadership

Brands, clients and complex communications.

Agency & Consulting

Before AT&T, Jodi spent more than a decade in agency and consulting environments, including leadership roles at HL Group, JGA Peppercomm and BLJ Worldwide.

Her work ranged from communications strategy and media relations to new-business development, product and restaurant launches, executive counsel and media training.

Over the course of her career, she has worked with organizations and brands including American Express, Expedia, Mayo Clinic, AARP, Samsung, DirecTV, The Capital Grille, CreditCards.com, SodaStream, Mustela, Platinum Guild International and Time Inc.

She has also prepared executives, physicians, authors, chefs and other spokespeople to communicate effectively with media and their audiences.

Public affairs

Government, nonprofit and public-facing work.

Jodi’s early career included work in government and public affairs, including serving as a Legislative Correspondent in the United States Senate and communications work connected with the Office of the Governor of Louisiana.

Her nonprofit and international communications experience has included organizations and initiatives focused on children with special needs, literacy, conservation and education.

Organizational Insight

Seeing the system,
not just the message.

More recently, Jodi worked inside public education, including special education classrooms and school communities.

That experience added another dimension to her communications work: firsthand insight into how policies, processes and decisions affect the people expected to carry them out.

Today, through Bragg Comm, she brings those experiences together to help organizations identify communication problems—including the ones that aren’t really communication problems at all.

“Listen carefully. Ask direct questions. Find the friction. Then get to the real issue.”

Jodi’s approach is practical and people-centered: helping teams clarify priorities, reduce unnecessary complexity and communicate in ways that make people feel respected, informed and able to move forward.

Her work is grounded in the belief that strong communication begins with understanding people, patterns and the systems shaping their decisions.
